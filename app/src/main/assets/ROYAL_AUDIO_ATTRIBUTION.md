This app includes an adapted one-shot owl-call recording.

Original recording: “Owlcall.ogg” by L. Shyamal, recorded near Wynaad, Kerala.
Original source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Owlcall.ogg
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for ROYAL AUDIO: trimmed to a short 6-second call, level-adjusted,
resampled to 48 kHz stereo PCM 24-bit WAV, and exported as OGG Vorbis.

The adapted owl audio asset is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
The recording's species identification was not visually confirmed by its contributor.
